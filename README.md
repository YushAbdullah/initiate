# INITIATE

A free browser game about building a product for real. Take a scattered idea from a napkin sketch through a Business Model Canvas, a live pitch, PM, Product Owner, analyst, UI/UX, Scrum sprints with CI/CD, QA, UAT, a real-time launch day war room, and a beta with real users. Then share your report card.

**Play:** https://initiate-something.pages.dev/

Also on GitHub Pages: https://yushabdullah.github.io/initiate/

Seven projects to choose from, including an AI assistant, an HR app, a dashboard and an e-commerce store.

The whole game is one self-contained file, `index.html`. Progress is saved only in your own browser.
